# Ostraka

A free Koine Greek study app for vocabulary and paradigms: spaced-repetition review,
mastery-based study sessions (flashcards, typing, multiple choice, English → Greek,
drawing, matching board), and 68 built-in paradigm charts.

**Use it:** open the site in any browser. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share → Add to Home Screen
to install it like an app (it works offline after the first visit).

Words and progress are saved on your own device. Add your own vocabulary under **Add Words**.
